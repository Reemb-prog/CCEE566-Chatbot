# Feedback sentiment / ABSA: evaluation (phase F3)

Generated by `scripts/eval_feedback.py`. Thresholds and model choices are set on training data only. Scores are agreement with the stated gold labels, not calibrated probabilities. Macro-F1 averages positive, negative and neutral equally.

> **The 40 human sentences are no longer a sealed test.** On 2026-11-21 the team chose to fix the errors found on them: spelling correction toward aspect words, 83 generated patch sentences (`RHU_synthetic_patch_f3.csv`), and a generated-phrase weight in the polarity model (chosen by train CV). The human-test numbers below are therefore *development* numbers. The last independent result is in `eval/feedback_results_sealed_v1.md`. EduRABSA test, S1 test and synthetic_dev were not used for these fixes and remain held out.

## Limits

- 40 human sentences from 11 respondents: this shows behaviour, not a reliable accuracy. Per-aspect counts are 1 to 8.
- Review-level labels are star ratings (weak labels). With rating 3 = neutral there are only 29 neutral test reviews.
- Most RHU aspects were learned from about 20 generated sentences each.
- The public review data is North-American course/teacher reviews.
